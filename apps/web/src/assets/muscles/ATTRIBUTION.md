`body-front.svg`, `body-back.svg`, and the highlight paths in
`src/data/muscle-regions.ts` are adapted from:

- [Muscular system.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Muscular_system.svg)
  and [Muscular system-back.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Muscular_system-back.svg)

  [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- The muscle overlays published by the [wger project](https://github.com/wger-project/wger)
  (`wger/core/static/images/muscles`), which are derived from the files above.

reduced precision, and the forearm regions drawn for FitnessApp. These adapted
files are distributed under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 license.
